## The problem

The goal was to turn raw coffee sales tables into an interactive dashboard that could be explored without add-ins.

## The data

The workbook contains three source tables — `orders`, `customers` and `products` — joined with lookups.

## The approach

The `Dashboard` sheet is driven by three pivot-backed views: `TotalSales` over time, `CountryBarChart` and `Top5Customers`. A date timeline controls the period, while slicers cover loyalty-card status, roast type and pack size.

## Results

The result is an interactive dashboard with a timeline plus three slicers driving four linked views. That is real dashboard modelling thinking, just in Excel: cleaning, pivots, dashboarding and linked controls.

## What I would do differently

- I would export screenshots of the dashboard with the slicers in different states when those assets are available.
- I would keep the workbook structure visible as evidence of the modelling work behind the dashboard.
